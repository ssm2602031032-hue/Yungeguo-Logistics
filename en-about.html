About our approach

YGG focuses on the international movement of drone cargo, with coordination from the initial enquiry through delivery for aircraft, batteries and parts.

The process starts with the cargo: model, intended use, battery status and destination form the basis of an assessment. Transport options and service scope are discussed for the actual shipment.

This website explains the information and communication needed at each stage, helping senders and recipients prepare documents, packing and delivery arrangements.

Our working principles

Check carefully

Identify missing conditions and important cargo details before proceeding.

Be clear

Use defined lists and scope descriptions instead of broad assurances.

Coordinate clearly

Help senders, recipients and operations understand their responsibilities.

Stay in communication

Retain the agreed details and review them when cargo, dates or destinations change.

A clear process from assessment to delivery

Four stages and eight steps clarify the information you provide and the work at each stage.

01

Assessment

01 Cargo enquiry

Share the model, intended use, battery status and destination, including unknown details.

02 Plan comparison

Compare channels, timing scope and charge items for the cargo, and define the service scope.

02

Preparation

03 Document review

Review descriptions, quantities, values and sender and recipient details; identify missing information.

04 Packing confirmation

Confirm restraint and protection, and record the packing list, measurements and photographs.

03

Transport

05 Dispatch coordination

Hand over the cargo under the agreed plan and coordinate dispatch information and handover records.

06 International movement

The contact shares available milestone updates and communicates changes or exceptions.

04

Delivery

07 Arrival & delivery

Coordinate destination information and receiving arrangements within the agreed delivery scope.

08 Receipt & follow-up

Confirm receipt, retain exception records and discuss any necessary follow-up.
