Six common questions before exporting a drone

01

How should I describe the aircraft?

Identify camera, industrial, agricultural or FPV aircraft and provide the exact model and intended use.

02

Can a shipment include batteries?

Identify installed, accompanying or separate batteries, then review the labels, packing and available documents.

03

Which information should I prepare?

Start with item details, quantity and value, and sender and recipient information. Add cargo-specific details after review.

04

How should the drone and gimbal be protected?

Discuss fragile areas and restraint, and retain photographs and lists before and after sealing.

05

Which transport option fits?

Compare shipment size, budget, target dates and delivery conditions rather than a single transit number.

06

What is included at the destination?

Confirm the address, document responsibilities and final delivery scope, with clear handover points.

Frequently asked questions

Can my drone be shipped?

Acceptance depends on the model, intended use, batteries, origin and destination. Share what you know and mark missing details as “to be confirmed”. A listed cargo category does not establish acceptance for an individual model.

What if I do not know the battery details?

Start with the product model and photographs of the battery label and packaging. State whether it is installed in the equipment. Avoid guessing specifications from a similar product; the required information can be clarified next.

What information is needed for a quote?

Prepare the description and model, number of packages, available weight and dimensions, origin, destination and battery status. The plan should state included services, allocation of duties and taxes, and final delivery scope.

Does a route have a fixed delivery time?

Timing varies by channel, cargo and delivery city. The confirmed plan should explain the starting and ending milestones, working or calendar days, and whether clearance and final delivery are included.

Can the original packaging be used?

Share photographs, dimensions and details of how the equipment is secured. Extra cushioning, reinforcement or an outer carton should be assessed for the cargo and transport method. Keep records before and after packing.

How can I follow the shipment or report a problem?

Use the telephone or email on the contact page and include the confirmed reference, sender and recipient details, and the issue. Your contact can check milestone updates and next steps. The website does not offer an unconnected live tracking service.
