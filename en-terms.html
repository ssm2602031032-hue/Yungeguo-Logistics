Purpose of this website

This website introduces shipment preparation and logistics coordination for drones, batteries and parts. Cargo categories and transport modes help structure a discussion; acceptance of an individual model depends on the cargo, documents and destination.

Confirm before dispatch

Confirm the description and model, quantity and value, batteries and packing, sender and recipient details, charges, timing scope and delivery responsibilities. Website information does not replace agreement on an individual shipment plan.

Timing and charges

Route maps are directional illustrations. Timing and departures are confirmed for the individual plan. Distinguish international transport, clearance and final delivery, and clarify responsibilities for duties, taxes and additional services.

Local preview functionality

The form can save and download an inquiry in the current browser. This does not mean that the company has received an order, accepted cargo or entered a transport agreement. Use the listed telephone or email for an actual discussion.

If an issue occurs

Retain the agreed details and packing and handover records, and contact the person handling the shipment. Follow-up should be discussed using the confirmed scope and actual circumstances.

Contact YGG

Export transport for drones, batteries and parts, with clear documentation, suitable routing and shipment updates.
